HOW FIRED COUNTS

Evidence before estimates.

One event, one canonical record, multiple corroborating sources. Figures are never silently promoted from estimates to confirmed counts.

CONFIRMED

Company announcement, regulatory filing or official government notice.

REPORTED

Credible newsroom or independently corroborated employee reporting.

ESTIMATED

Approximation or range reported by a source; uncertainty remains visible.

UNDISCLOSED

Layoff event is tracked, but a reliable headcount is not available.
